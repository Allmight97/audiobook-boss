# Frontend Directives

## Routing

- Owner rules and engine pointers: `src/app/AGENTS.md`. View traps:
  `src/ui/AGENTS.md`.
- IPC adaptation, `tauriClient`, and generated bindings:
  `src/lib/tauri/AGENTS.md`.
- Provider secrets and raw provider payloads stay backend-only.
